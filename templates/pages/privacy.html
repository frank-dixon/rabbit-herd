Privacy policy

Last updated {{ updated }}

{{ app_name }} is a free app for keeping records on your rabbits. This page explains what we store, why, and what you can ask us to do with it.

What we store

  • Your account: your email address and a securely hashed password, or a passkey. We never see or store your password itself.
  • Your herd records: rabbits, pedigrees, litters, weights, health notes, tasks, costs, contacts and anything else you type in.
  • Photos you upload of your rabbits. They are stored privately and only shown to you when signed in.
  • Guest sessions: if you try the app without an account, your herd is tied to a cookie on that device until you create an account.
  • Basic technical logs (such as IP address and browser type) that our hosting providers keep for security and to fix errors.

How we use it

Only to run the app for you: to show your records, sign you in, send account emails (confirming your email, resetting your password, reminders you turn on) and keep the service secure. We don't use your data for advertising.

We don't sell your data

We don't sell, rent or trade your information. We share it only with the services that host the app for us (web hosting, database and photo storage, and email delivery), and only so they can run it.

Cookies

We use only the cookies needed to keep you signed in and to protect forms. No tracking or advertising cookies.

Your choices

  • Export: download your records as CSV files from Settings at any time.
  • Delete your account: email {% include "pages/_legal_contact.html" %} from the address on your account and we'll delete your account, records and photos.
  • Correct: you can edit or delete any record yourself in the app.

Security and backups

Connections use HTTPS and passwords are hashed. We keep backups so we can recover from mistakes. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a unique password.

Children

The app isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above, and we'll email you if a change is significant.
