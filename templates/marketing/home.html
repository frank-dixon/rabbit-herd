What it does, as it looks in the app.

Every picture on this page is a screenshot of {{ app_name }} with its demo herd.

Family tree

Trace a gene through five generations.

Pick a gene and the tree marks every rabbit that carries it or shows it. Solid links are proven by kits; dashed ones are probable.

Weights

Every weight, with a trend and a growth check.

Each rabbit shows its latest weight, the 30-day change and a small trend line. Juniors are checked against a New Zealand growth curve.

Litters

Litters on schedule.

Each litter shows when it was bred, when it is due and the next job, with dates from your breeding schedule.

Pairings

Know how related a pair is before you breed.

Both pedigrees are read for you. You get the inbreeding coefficient and every shared ancestor, on each side.

Rabbit records

One page for each rabbit.

Photos, breed, color, line and the whole weight history, with the main photo up front.

Offline

Keeps working without signal.

Log a weight in the barn with no connection. It is kept on the phone and added once when you are back online.

Bring your whole herd over in an afternoon.
